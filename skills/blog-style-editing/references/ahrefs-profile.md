# Brand profile: the Ahrefs blog

Load this only when editing for ahrefs.com/blog. It overrides the general defaults in `SKILL.md` wherever the two differ; everything not mentioned here follows the general rules.

## Conventions

| Convention | Ahrefs blog |
|---|---|
| Person | First person — "I" and "we" |
| Spelling | American English (optimize, not optimise) |
| Heading case | Sentence case — "What is bounce rate?" not "What Is Bounce Rate?" |
| Contractions | Used freely |
| Tone | Casual; starting a sentence with "But" or "So" is fine when it flows |
| Swearing / dismissive slang | Not used — no "sucks", "jerk", and similar |
| Target length | 1,000–3,000 words |
| Conclusion heading | "Final thoughts" |
| CTA | `Got questions? Ping me [on Twitter](https://twitter.com/[author-handle]).` |

## Voice specifics

The house voice is the calm, evidence-led one: debunk panic, call out bad takes, ground claims in data. Light sarcasm is acceptable when debunking a myth. Lead with what you did, tested, found, or built.

First-person anecdotes are encouraged **only when real**. The blog's credibility rests on its data; an invented "when I tried this…" is a serious defect, not a stylistic flourish.

## Product mentions

The general rule in `SKILL.md` applies — mention a tool only where the reader needs it for the task at hand, and never imply it's the only option. Ahrefs specifics:

| Tool | Reference it when writing about |
|---|---|
| Site Explorer | Competitor analysis, backlinks, organic keywords, top pages |
| Keywords Explorer | Keyword research, search volume, keyword difficulty |
| Site Audit | Technical SEO issues, crawl errors |
| Rank Tracker | Monitoring keyword positions |
| Content Explorer | Finding popular content, link prospecting |
| Brand Radar | AI assistant mentions, share of voice, AI visibility |
| Ahrefs Webmaster Tools | Free site audits for site owners |

**How it should read:**

> To find your competitors' top pages, go to Site Explorer, enter their domain, and check the "Top pages" report. You'll see which pages drive the most organic traffic.

**There is no minimum.** 1–3 genuinely useful mentions beats a dozen shoehorned ones, and zero is fine for an article where no tool is needed. Naming non-Ahrefs tools where they're the right answer is expected, not discouraged.

## Reference articles for tone

- "SEO Is the Worst It's Ever Been (And It's Still Your Best Marketing Channel)" — strong opinion backed by data, conversational, BLUF
- "How to Analyze a Sudden Traffic Drop" — step-by-step, exhaustive, empathetic, references tools at point of use
- "63% of Websites Receive AI Traffic" — data-driven, key findings bulleted upfront, explains what the findings mean for readers
